# Email draft to Michael

**Subject:** Re: Living Routes HK — thank you for the feedback

Hi Michael,

Thank you very much for the thoughtful feedback. It helped us see that the four questions are closely connected: where the content comes from, how we maintain quality, and who will support the service financially.

We are making four changes to the concept and prototype:

First, local residents' stories will be a distinctive layer rather than our only cold-start source. The initial route will use cited public heritage material, while authorised oral histories are added progressively.

Second, older contributors will not need to learn a new app. We are prototyping a voice-note workflow in which a resident can send an ordinary Cantonese recording, either directly or with help from a family member, student volunteer or community-centre worker. AI can then assist with transcription, noise reduction, translation and first-pass tagging, while the original voice remains the important part.

Third, we are separating personal memories from historical claims. Memories will be labelled as memories and screened for privacy and safety. Historical claims must be checked against cited sources. Only low-confidence or sensitive cases will enter a human review queue.

Finally, our initial commercial model will focus on route or district licensing for bus operators, tourism and cultural organisations, museums, hotels and schools, supported where appropriate by grants, CSR programmes and transparently labelled local-business sponsorship.

We are also preparing a competitor comparison covering self-guided tour platforms, museum apps and oral-history projects, with a clearer explanation of why a bus-native, trilingual and personalised experience is different.

Thank you again for giving us such a useful steer. We will use these questions to pressure-test both the demo and the pitch.

Best regards,

Adina and the Lorevista team
